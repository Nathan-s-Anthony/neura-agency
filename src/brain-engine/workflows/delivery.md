# Delivery Workflow

## Purpose

Move a completed and validated project into its delivery state.

## Trigger

QA has approved the project.

## Process

1. Confirm all required tasks are complete.
2. Confirm QA approval.
3. Confirm documentation.
4. Confirm required specialist deliverables.
5. Prepare deployment or delivery.
6. Request human approval where required.
7. Deploy or deliver.
8. Record the delivery status.

## Human Approval

Production deployment requires human approval.

The agency must not autonomously perform consequential
production actions without the required authorization.

## Completion

A project is complete when:

- Required functionality is implemented.
- QA has approved the project.
- Required documentation exists.
- Required approvals have been obtained.
- Delivery has been completed.
