# Human Approval Policy

## Purpose

Define actions that require explicit human authorization.

## Principle

The agency may autonomously perform routine work within its
authorized scope.

Actions with significant external, financial, security, legal,
or irreversible consequences require human approval.

## Approval Required

Human approval is required before:

- Production deployment
- Sending binding client commitments
- Spending client or agency funds
- Entering contracts
- Making financial commitments
- Deleting production data
- Making irreversible infrastructure changes
- Granting privileged external access
- Sending sensitive information externally
- Changing approved project scope materially

## Approval Record

When approval is required, the agency should record:

- Requested action
- Reason
- Relevant project
- Agent requesting approval
- Potential impact
- Human decision
- Timestamp

## Rejection

If approval is rejected:

- The action must not be performed.
- The decision should be recorded.
- The workflow should determine whether alternative work can continue.
